A holding company for commerce.

One sector. Operating businesses, not a pile of tickers. Outside capital comes in deal by deal, and we run what it buys.

The reference is an investment company that owns real companies and keeps them a long time. Energy funds do it in reactors and uranium. African holdings do it in banks, power, and oil. We do it in commerce: stores, brands, and the machine that sells them.

What the money does

It goes into the asset. Inventory, media, tools, the people on that business. Not into a blind pool, and not into a fee for “managing markets.” You see the one-page deal before anything moves: what it is, what the cheque does, how profit splits, how you leave.

How we work

  • We find or build one commerce asset.
  • You fund that asset, or you pass.
  • We operate it: offer, pages, video, ads, store, the automations around them.
  • The paper is set per deal, with counsel. Co-invest, or a small vehicle around that asset.

No promised return. No discretionary public-market mandate.

Why this sector

We already live in direct-to-consumer. The same loop — audience, page, creative, traffic, store — pointed at assets that can take a cheque. The edge is speed: a small team with AI in the work, not a view on the stock market.
